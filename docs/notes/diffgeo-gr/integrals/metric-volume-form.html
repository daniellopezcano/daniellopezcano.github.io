8.3 The Metric Volume Form

Chapter 8 • Integrals

8.3 The Metric Volume Form

  • Everything above holds for any volume form. On an oriented metric manifold \((\mathcal{M}, \mathcal{O}, \mathcal{A}^\uparrow, g)\), the metric singles out one, the metric volume form \(\Omega^g\), whose components in every chart \((\mathcal{U}, \mathscr{X}) \in \mathcal{A}^\uparrow\) are \begin{equation} \big( \Omega^g_\mathscr{X} \big)_{i_1 \dots i_d} \coloneqq \sqrt{ \big| \det g_\mathscr{X} \big| } \; \varepsilon_{i_1 \dots i_d}, \qquad \det g_\mathscr{X} \coloneqq \det \big( (g_\mathscr{X})_{ij} \big) \in C^\infty(\mathcal{U}), \tag{8.5} \end{equation} i.e. whose scalar density is \(\omega^g_\mathscr{X} = \sqrt{|\det g_\mathscr{X}|}\), smooth and positive since \(g\) is non-degenerate. On \(\mathcal{U} \cap \mathcal{V}\), with \((\mathcal{U}, \mathscr{X}), (\mathcal{V}, \mathscr{Y}) \in \mathcal{A}^\uparrow\), these components transform as those of a \((0,d)\)-tensor field9: \begin{align*} \big( \Omega^g_\mathscr{Y} \big)_{j_1 \dots j_d}& \coloneqq \sqrt{\big| \det g_\mathscr{Y} \big|} \; \varepsilon_{j_1 \dots j_d} \, \overset{\text{Ch.~7}}{=} \, \sqrt{\Big| \det \Big( \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^{i}_{k} \, \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^{j}_{l} \, (g_\mathscr{X})_{ij} \Big) \Big|} \; \varepsilon_{j_1 \dots j_d} \xlongrightarrow[\text{transpose: same } \det]{\det \text{ multiplicative}} ... \\ ... = &\sqrt{\det \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^2} \, \sqrt{\big| \det g_\mathscr{X} \big|} \; \varepsilon_{j_1 \dots j_d} = \operatorname{sgn}\big( \det \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big) \big) \, \sqrt{\big| \det g_\mathscr{X} \big|} \; \det \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big) \; \varepsilon_{j_1 \dots j_d} \xlongrightarrow{\text{(c)}} ... \\ ... = &\underbrace{\operatorname{sgn}\big( \det \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big) \big)}_{= \, +1 \text{ in } \mathcal{A}^\uparrow} \; \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^{i_1}_{j_1} \cdots \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^{i_d}_{j_d} \, \underbrace{\sqrt{\big| \det g_\mathscr{X} \big|} \; \varepsilon_{i_1 \dots i_d}}_{( \Omega^g_\mathscr{X} )_{i_1 \dots i_d}} = \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^{i_1}_{j_1} \cdots \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^{i_d}_{j_d} \, \big( \Omega^g_\mathscr{X} \big)_{i_1 \dots i_d} . \end{align*} The chart-wise definitions thus agree on overlaps and define a single \((0,d)\)-tensor field \(\Omega^g\), totally antisymmetric, as \(\varepsilon\) is, and nowhere vanishing: a volume form. Without the orientation, the two definitions would differ by a sign wherever \(\det \big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big) < 0\). If the chart-induced basis at \(p\) is orthonormal in the sense of Sylvester’s law of inertia, \(g_{ij}(p) = 0\) for \(i \neq j\) and \(g_{ii}(p) \in \{+1, -1\}\) (Chapter 7), then \(\omega^g_\mathscr{X}(p) = 1\): \(\Omega^g\) assigns unit volume to the parallelepiped spanned by an orthonormal basis.

    With \(\omega^g_\mathscr{X} = \sqrt{|\det g_\mathscr{X}|}\), the integrals (8.3) and (8.4) become, writing \(\int_\mathcal{M} f \coloneqq \int_\mathcal{M} \Omega^g f\), \[ \int_\mathcal{M} f = \sum_n \int_{\mathscr{X}_n(\mathcal{U}_n)} d^d\alpha \, \sqrt{ \big| \det g_{\mathscr{X}_n} \big| \big( \mathscr{X}_n^{-1}(\alpha) \big) } \; \big( (\textrm{\runic ᚹ}_n f) \circ \mathscr{X}_n^{-1} \big)(\alpha) , \] valid for any signature.

9

The determinants are those of the matrices of components: \(\det g_\mathscr{X}\) is a function on \(\mathcal{U}\), not a scalar field, and picks up \(\det\big( \textrm{\runic ᛞ}_{\mathcal{U}\mathcal{V}} \big)^2\) under a change of chart.
