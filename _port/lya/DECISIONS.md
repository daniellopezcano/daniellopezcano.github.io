## Architecture

**L9. One converter, configured per course.** `_port/convert/courses.py` holds one entry per course (directory, notes path and its override variable, scratch directory, TeX engine, files to copy, heading levels, label prefixes, landing-page builder, cut, which generated pages exist, accent stylesheet, interactive panels file). `build.py --course <name>` builds one; `build.py` with no `--course` builds every course in turn, each in its own process (the converter keeps per-run module state). Every first-course constant moved into its entry with its value unchanged, so the first course's pages stay byte-identical (checked by `_port/tools/course_unchanged.py`).

**L10. Heading levels.** The first notes are a `book` (chapter → page with the opening text, section → page, subsection/subsubsection → `<h2>`/`<h3>`). These notes are an `article`: `\section` plays the chapter's role (an opening page with the text before its first subsection, and the computed list of its subsections), `\subsection` the section's (one page each), `\subsubsection` → `<h2>`, `\paragraph` → `<h3>` (unnumbered run-in heading, as `article` sets it). The page kinds inside the converter stay `chapter`/`section`; the words shown are the notes' ("Section 3", "Appendix B" in the kicker). This keeps both courses on one code path and gives pages of 1–6 screens (§ 3 alone is 20 PDF pages).

**L11. Shared design files are copied, not re-written.** The design system's hand-written files live where the first course has them (`notes/diffgeo-gr/assets/`: `course.css`, `course.js`, `interactive.css`, `interactive/dg.js`, `fonts/newcm-*.woff2`). The converter copies them byte for byte into `notes/lya-forest/assets/` (generated copies; `check.sh` verifies they are identical). Why: `course.js` finds its course from its own URL (`import.meta.url`), so a copy works without a code change, and the first course's pages keep loading exactly what they load today. Course-specific files of the second course are hand-written in its own `assets/`: `mathjax-config.js` (its macros), `lya.css` (accent and cover), `interactive/*.js` (its panels).

**L12. Numbering hooks also wrap the counter steps that packages call directly** (`_port/tex/port-hooks.tex`). With the Lyα preamble the first scratch build recorded only equations, list items and footnotes: `titlesec` steps sections through its own copy `\ttl@Hy@refstepcounter`, and `hyperref`'s `\caption` (no `caption` package here) through `\H@refstepcounter`, both taken before the hook was installed. The hooks now wrap `\refstepcounter`, `\H@refstepcounter`, `\ttl@Hy@refstepcounter` and `\caption@@refstepcounter`, each through `\port@wrap`, which logs a step only at the outermost hooked call (hyperref's `\refstepcounter` calls `\H@refstepcounter`, so without the guard every step would be logged twice). Verified: the first course's `main.port` is byte-identical with the new hooks (538 records); the Lyα build records 296 (sections, subsections, subsubsections, figures, tables, equations, footnotes, list items), and its labels equal the author's `main.aux`; `pdftotext` of the scratch PDF equals the author's.

**L13. Constructs taught to the converter for these notes** (all additive: the first course uses none of them, and its pages are unchanged).
- `\paragraph{..}` → an unnumbered `<h3>` (article's `secnumdepth` 3 numbers nothing below subsubsection; the run-in heading becomes a heading of the page outline and the search index).
- `\AA` → Å (text mode; inside math the math configuration handles it, L4 unit).
- `table` float → `<figure class="dg-table-float">` with the tabular and "Table N:" + caption where the source puts them; `\centering`, `\renewcommand{\arraystretch}`, `\setlength{\tabcolsep}` inside it apply to the tabular; anything else stops the page. Numbered from `main.port` (counter `table`).
- In a tabular cell: `\multicolumn{n}{spec}{..}` (colspan; the cell's alignment and rules from its spec), `\multirow{n}{*}{..}` (rowspan; the covered cells below must be empty in the source), `\cellcolor{c}` (background), and the preamble's macros of the shape `\newcommand{\X}[1]{\cellcolor{c}{#1}}` (`\TotZero`…`\TotSix`), recognised by that shape from the preamble (a macro of another shape stays unknown and stops the page).
- Colours `c!p` with xcolor's base names (cyan, magenta, yellow, green, orange, violet, …) are mixed with the paper in CSS (`color-mix`), exactly what xcolor computes on white; the tokens are xcolor's RGB values (`course.css`). In the dark theme the same mix with the dark paper gives dark tints of the same hues.
- Table lengths in em of the notes' type size (`courses.py` `pt`: 10 for the first notes, 11 here).

- *Toy sightlines* (`interactive/skewer.js`, panels 2, 3, 7). The notes take n, T and v from a simulation (Figure 2); a panel needs gas that a browser can make in milliseconds, so the toy is stated in the module and in the panel's REVIEW entry: a Gaussian field g (lya.js), density 1 + δ = exp(g − σ_g²/2) (σ_g = 0.7, ℓ = 0.5 Mpc), n_com = n̄ (1 + δ)^1.6, T = T0 (1 + δ)^0.6 (T0 = 1.5·10⁴ K), and the linear-theory velocity ∂v/∂x = −aHg (f = 1), along 40 Mpc (100 Mpc for panel 7) ending at z = 2.4, a(x) from Eq. (2.5) for Planck 2018 (Figure 1's). Everything from the gas to τ is the notes': Eq. (tau_straight_los) on cells, the local wavelength of Eq. (wavelength_cosmological_z_and_doppler) in the exact velocity units of Eq. (velocity_units_normalized), V of § 3.3, the widths of § 3.1.3, C_j = e² f_j/(4ε0 m_e c). n̄ = 5·10⁻⁷ m⁻³ gives a mean transmission of about 0.8 for Lyα (typical at z ≈ 2.4). Each cell's profile is summed over the pixels within 15 Doppler widths of its resonance; the rest is the far Lorentzian wings.
