## Interactive figures

The test is the same as for the first course: what does a student understand by manipulating this that the static figure cannot show? Build one consistent panel per idea, compact and next to the concept, with the static figure always available. Candidates to confirm against the source:

- Evolution of the scale factor along a photon's path, for the background cosmologies the notes treat, including the analytic solutions.
- Line profile: Gaussian, Lorentzian and Voigt, driven by temperature, the b parameter and the damping constant, in the notes' notation.
- Mapping observed wavelength to the local gas frame: cosmological redshift plus Doppler shift, and the exact versus approximate velocity units.
- Optical depth along a sightline from a toy density, temperature and velocity field, showing how peculiar velocities move and sharpen the absorption.
- Transmission F = e^{-τ}: saturation, the mean flux, and flux fluctuations δF.
- The 1D 2PCF: per-sightline estimate versus ensemble average as sightlines are added (ergodicity), and its decomposition into components.

Rank them in `_port/lya/PLAN.md` and build the best six to eight.
